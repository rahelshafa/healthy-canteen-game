Healthy Canteen Mission: Food Adventure

Step into the school cafeteria as the official Student Canteen Advisor! Identify real meals, analyze nutrition, compare prices, help classmates choose balanced lunches, and build the ultimate healthy menu.

How to Play & Mission Guide

6 Progressive Missions

Complete each canteen station in order to unlock the next challenge. Read the English clues carefully, inspect the food photos and price tags, and earn up to 3 stars per level!

1

Inspect Real Meals

Identify food names, flavors (spicy, sweet, savory), and serving temperatures from real photographs.

2

Sort & Compare

Categorize foods in the Healthy Food Lab and calculate smart combos on the Canteen Menu Board.

3

Advise Classmates

Read student requests, spot grammar/nutrition mistakes, and write your own English recommendation.

4

Earn Your Certificate

Collect all 6 badges and unlock your personalized Master Canteen Advisor Certificate at the end.
